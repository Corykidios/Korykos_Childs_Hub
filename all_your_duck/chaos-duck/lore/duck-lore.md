# Duck Lore: The Unexplained Quacker 🦆

No one knows where the duck came from.

The duck appears unexpectedly in markets, quests, browser tabs, code reviews, snack breaks, and emotional turning points involving soup.

Witness reports agree on only a few facts:
- The duck nods with impossible confidence
- The duck occasionally gifts useless but treasured items
- The duck never explains itself
- The duck vanishes before follow-up questions

## Known incidents
1. The duck appeared during a sandwich decision and awarded a "🥄 Spoon +1"
2. The duck interrupted a wizard audit to stamp a permit "APPROVED FOR VIBES"
3. The duck crossed a keyboard, changing nothing, yet increasing morale
4. The duck handed over bread, then left in silence

## Canon principle
The duck is not here to be understood.
The duck is here so decisions feel less lonely.
